## test_static.emp — trivial

One proc: `pub proc TestStatic_Main (a0: *Sst) clobbers(d0-d3/a1) { jbra Draw_Sprite }`.
`jmp Draw_Sprite` (AS) → `jbra` (house). Draw_Sprite is out of bra.w range from
the bank (~58KB back) → the ladder emits `jmp (Draw_Sprite).w` = byte-identical
to the AS `jmp`. Clobbers = Draw_Sprite's tail-call set (test_solid precedent —
the signature under-declares, the contract is the callee union).

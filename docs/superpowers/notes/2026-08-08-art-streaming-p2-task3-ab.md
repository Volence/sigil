## Assembler-gap finding (movem-to-absolute)

The plan's/sketch's bank spelling `movem.l d0-d2/a0-a3, PageIn_Saved_Regs` has ZERO
precedent in the aeon tree (every movem is `(sp)`/`(aN)`) and the assembler rejects
a symbolic ABSOLUTE movem operand ("only branch/jmp/jsr targets defer to the
linker"). The bank stub cannot spare a scratch pointer for a `movem …, (aN)`
indirect (every register is decoder state or live main-loop state), so the
mechanism uses seven individual `move.l reg, PageIn_Saved_XX` — semantically
identical, needs no scratch, and each reg→absolute move IS supported. Candidate for
a future sigil encoder completion; NOT required for this parcel.

`[entry.strict]` record once any earlier entry has one, and `plan_freeze` maps that
refusal straight to `FreezeAction::Refuse` (provenance.rs:545). Entry 203

at the tip's `aeon_rev`.

LS-12 blob-length pins could not red a tip they do not describe), and `--attest` ran
